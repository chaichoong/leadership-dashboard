01

Start where you are: overwhelmed

Everything ran through one person: eleven staff, every inbox, every invoice, every decision. The business stopped when the founder stopped. Nothing about this is unusual. This is where most owners live.

02

Get the business into one place

Tasks, money, customers and properties moved into one system everyone reads, out of heads, inboxes and spreadsheets. An agent cannot run a job it cannot see. This is the foundation, and most businesses skip it.

03

Capture how each job is done

You record a short screen video of yourself doing the job, talking as you go. That recording becomes the written process. Nobody types a manual, and nothing lives only in your head any more.

04

Give the AI your business's memory

Decisions, people, preferences and meetings feed a business brain every night. An agent with no context is a temp on day one. An agent with your memory works like someone who has been there for years.

05

One agent, safest job first

The first agent takes a job that happens every day and is easy to undo if wrong. Ours was bank reconciliation. It proposed, the owner approved every line, and it was live inside the first month.

06

Approve, correct, remember

Every yes or no is scored. Every no is remembered, so the same mistake is not made twice. Accuracy earns autonomy: you move the gear, the agent never promotes itself. If accuracy slips, approvals switch back on.

07

An agent for every job, measured in hours

Each repeatable job gets its own agent with a goal, a clock and a guardrail. One number on the dashboard: the share of listed hours now run by agents. Watch it climb, take the next job off the list, and keep going until it reads 90%.

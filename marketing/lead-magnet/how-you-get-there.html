01

List every job

Every repeatable job in the business goes on one list: what gets done, how often, and how long it takes. That list is the denominator of the 90%. Nothing is automated that is not on it.

02

Rank by how often, and how safe

Each job is placed on a simple grid: how often it happens against what a mistake would cost. The first agents come from the often-and-safe corner: done every day, easy to undo if wrong.

03

Record it, do not write it

You record a short screen video of yourself doing the job, answering seven on-screen prompts as you go. That recording becomes the written process and the agent's instructions. Nobody types a manual.

04

Build the agent

From the recording we build the agent: when it starts, what it reads, the rules it follows, the tools it uses, what it must never do, and what finished looks like. Your first agent is live within the first month, in approve-everything mode.

05

Move the gear, when you are ready

Four gears, and you hold the dial: guardrails set, approve everything, loosen the leash, heartbeat. It never promotes itself. If its accuracy slips, approvals switch back on by themselves.

06

Measure, then take the next job

One number on your dashboard: the share of your listed hours now run by agents. Watch it climb, take the next job off the list. Demand comes last: we do not turn the tap on until the business can take the water.

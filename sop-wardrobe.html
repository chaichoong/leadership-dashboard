Wardrobe

The "What to wear today" card on My Dashboard, and the Wardrobe page. Version 1.0, 27 October 2026.

What it is for

Each morning My Dashboard opens with today's day, date, place, the "feels like" temperature and the conditions, a one-line reason, and three outfits: Stay at home, Out casual and Out smart casual.

Press Wearing this on the one you put on. It saves today's date on that outfit and its clothes, so the card offers something else tomorrow.

The Wardrobe page lists the clothes you own, the recommended additions and what to clear out.

How the outfits are chosen

  1. For each option, the card takes an outfit from the Outfits table that is in season (October to March is autumn and winter) and whose clothes are all owned.
  2. The outfit worn longest ago comes first. Once you press Wearing, that outfit stays on the card for the rest of the day.
  3. The weather then adds the rest:
WhenOut casualOut smart casual
Feels-like low under 12°Adds a jacket: the rain jacket if rain is 50% or more, else the casual jacket—
Feels-like low under 10°—Adds the smart coat
Rain 50% or moreSwaps trainers for that option's leather boots
Low of 6° or belowAdds a beanie
Low of 4° or belowAdds a scarf

An outfit that already includes a jacket or coat gets no extra one. Stay at home never gets layers. The rules live in js/wardrobe-pick.js and are pinned by tests/wardrobe-pick.test.js.

Where the data lives

  • Wardrobe table: one row per piece. Status is Own, Bought, Need, Clear out or Skip. Layer role marks the pieces the weather adds (rain jacket, casual jacket, smart coat, beanie, scarf, leather boots).
  • Outfits table: one row per outfit, with its option, season and linked clothes.
  • Weather: Open-Meteo, free and with no key. It uses your location when the browser allows it, otherwise central Cambridge. Press "Use my location" on the card to switch.

To add a piece, add a Wardrobe row. To add an outfit, add an Outfits row, link its clothes and give it an Order number (lower comes first when two outfits are equally due; a row without one goes last). To retire a piece, set its Status to Clear out with an Action.

Health bar checks (Wardrobe page)

  • Wardrobe table read
  • Outfits table read
  • Every outfit links to clothes that exist
  • Today's weather read
  • An outfit ready for every option today

Troubleshooting

  • "No outfit ready for this season": every outfit for that option uses something not owned, or is out of season. Add or edit an Outfits row.
  • "Today's weather could not be read": the options still show, without weather layers. Press Refresh on the Wardrobe page's bar.
  • "That did not save": nothing changed in Airtable. Press Wearing again.

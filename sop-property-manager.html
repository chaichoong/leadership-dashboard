Property Manager

Roy Lavin's single page: the numbers he needs to bring the rent in and keep maintenance down.
SOP version 1.1 · page version 1.1 · 9 September 2026

1. Purpose

Roy is head of the property business. His two levers are rent in and maintenance spend out. This page gives him only what those two levers need: the portfolio, who is behind, the last 31 days of real money, a planned month, rent falling due, profit by property, and his tasks. Kevin's personal money, bank balances, debt, wealth and the AI agents never appear.

Live at https://app.operationsdirector.co.uk/property-manager/. Kevin sees the same page in the sidebar under Operations.

2. How the page gets its data

The Worker is the boundary

The page never talks to Airtable. A Cloudflare Worker (workers/property-manager/) holds the Airtable key server-side, reads only the property tables, and returns totals plus the task list. Two rules are enforced in the Worker, not the page:

  • Transactions are filtered at the query to Business (For Reports) = Real Estate. Personal and Operations Director transactions never reach the Worker's memory.
  • Running costs is the FULL fixed-cost total, the same set as the Leadership Dashboard's Monthly Costs (Kevin's ruling, 9 Sep 2026): some of Kevin's own fixed costs sit inside it and Roy needs the true cash-flow figure. The page says how many of the costs are not property costs. It never lists them.

Sign in

Roy and Kevin each have their own passcode, held as Worker secrets. A passcode gives a 12-hour signed session. Five wrong attempts a minute per address are refused. The passcodes live only at ~/.config/od/property_manager_passcode_* on Kevin's Mac and in the Worker. They are never in the repo.

4. Updating a task

  1. Click a task. The drawer shows the brief, the notes so far, the due date and the status.
  2. Change the due date, the status (Upcoming, Today or Completed) and/or add a note. Or press the green Complete button, on the card or in the drawer.
  3. Save. The Worker checks the task is on the property lane before writing. Anything else is refused.

A new due date sets the status the way the Tasks page does: past is Overdue, today is Today, later is Upcoming; Approval and Completed are left alone. Notes are appended with a date and the name of whoever saved them. Complete stamps the Completion Date from the Worker's clock and shows an Undo for eight seconds; Undo within fifteen minutes puts the task back where its date puts it and clears the stamp. After that a completed task cannot be reopened from this page.

5. Health checks

The strip at the top of the page runs seven checks after every load:

  • Property transactions loaded (fails at zero: a zero here is a broken query, never an empty month)
  • Tenancies and units loaded
  • Running costs use the dashboard rule (active fixed costs, same set as the Leadership Dashboard)
  • Rent collected has a source (transactions inside the 31-day window)
  • Profit by property allocates (warns when more than 10% of rent has no property link)
  • Task list matches the board (open property-lane tasks)
  • Feed is fresh (computed under 30 minutes ago; the Worker caches for 10)

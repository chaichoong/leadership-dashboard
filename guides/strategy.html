Objective & Strategy

Your guide to this page

This is your business plan on one page: where you want to be in the future, and the few things to focus on right now. A friendly AI coach asks you questions and writes the plan for you.

What this page is for

It holds your goals for 9 years, 3 years and 1 year ahead, plus the 3 most important projects for the next three months and the small monthly steps to get them done. Think of it as your map: where you're headed, and the next few turns to take.

What you can do here

  • Write down your long-term goals (9-year, 3-year, 1-year).
  • Choose the 3 big projects to focus on this quarter.
  • Break each project into 3 simple monthly steps.
  • Let the AI coach interview you and fill it all in.
  • Send your projects straight to your Tasks page so the work gets done.

How to fill in your plan

  1. At the top, pick your business, the quarter (which 3 months) and the year.
  2. Click AI Wizard. A chat opens on the right.
  3. Answer its questions in your own words. It may ask you to be more specific — that's normal. It just wants real numbers, dates and names so the plan is clear.
  4. When it's finished it fills in the whole plan. Read it through and change anything you like.
  5. Click Save changes. Done.
Tip: A good answer is clear enough that anyone could tell when it's finished — for example "get 10 new customers by 31 March", not "get more customers".

Every three months

At the start of each new quarter, pick the new quarter at the top and run the AI Wizard again. It remembers last time and starts by asking how things went — what worked and what didn't. Your old plans are always kept, so you can look back and compare what you planned with what you did.

Turning plans into action

After you save, the page can send your 3 projects to your Tasks & Projects page, and each monthly step becomes a task with a due date. When you tick a task off as done, it shows as complete here too — so your plan and your to-do list always match.

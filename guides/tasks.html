Tasks & Projects

Your guide to this page

This is your team's to-do list. Every job that needs doing lives here, so nothing gets forgotten and everyone knows what they're working on.

What this page is for

It keeps all your work in one place. Each task is one job to do. Tasks can be grouped under a bigger project, given to a team member, and given a due date. You can see everything as a simple list, or as a board where jobs move from left to right as they get done.

What you can do here

  • Add jobs that need doing and give each one a due date.
  • Hand a job to a team member so they know it's theirs.
  • Group related jobs under a project.
  • See what's to do, in progress and done at a glance.
  • Watch your plan turn into action — projects from your Strategy page show up here.

How to add a task

  1. Click the green + Add Task button (top right).
  2. Type what needs doing in a few words.
  3. Choose who it's for and when it's due.
  4. Save. It now appears in your list and on the board.

How to update a task

  1. Click a task to open it.
  2. Change anything — who's doing it, the due date, or mark it done.
  3. On the board, you can also drag a task from one column to the next as it moves along.
Tip: Keep task names short and clear — start with a doing word, like "Call the plumber" or "Send the invoice". Future-you will thank you.

The two views

List — every task in a row, best for a quick scan or ticking things off.
Board — columns like To do → Doing → Done. Drag a card across as work moves forward. Great for seeing where everything stands.

Where tasks come from

You can add tasks yourself here, and they also arrive automatically from your Objective & Strategy page — when you set your projects for the quarter, the monthly steps drop straight in here as tasks. Tick one off and your plan shows it as done too.

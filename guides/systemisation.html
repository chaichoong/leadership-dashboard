Systemisation

Your guide to this page

Write down how your business actually runs — step by step — so it can keep running smoothly even when you're not there.

What this page is for

It's a place to map out every process in your business, laid out along the journey a customer takes with you — from first hearing about you all the way to becoming a loyal regular. Once it's written down, anyone on your team can follow it.

What you can do here

  • See your whole business as one clear map, following the customer journey.
  • Break each stage into workflows — the actual steps you follow.
  • Write simple how-to notes so anyone can do the job.
  • Track how much of each stage you've written down.

The customer journey

The map is built around seven stages (they spell FULFILL):

  1. Find & Grab Attention — get noticed by the right people.
  2. Understand & Enquire — handle enquiries and check they're a good fit.
  3. Lead to Sales Process — win the work.
  4. Finance Received — take payment.
  5. Initiate Onboarding — welcome the new customer in.
  6. Launch Delivery — do the work you were paid for.
  7. Loyalty Programme — keep them coming back.

Your AI agents

The AI Agents tab is the home for your AI workforce. It shows three layers:

  • Role agents — agents with one specific job each (Prospecting, Reconciliation, Inbound Comms…). Each shows its goal, its guardrails, its status, how much work it has done, and its accuracy score.
  • Strategic layer — the AI CEO, department heads and workers. They give direction and fill the gaps.
  • Process agents — agents built from a recorded workflow video.

To create an agent, click + Create an agent. The AI asks you seven questions (the AGENTIC script) one at a time and checks each answer as you go:

  1. Aim — what it is for.
  2. Go Signal — what tells it to start. Each agent has its own trigger; there is no shared timetable.
  3. Entry Points — where it reads from.
  4. Navigate — the steps in order, how it decides the grey areas, and what it does when unsure (guess, skip, or ask you).
  5. Tools & Transformations — what it changes, creates or sends.
  6. Inspections & Caveats — the checks and the never-dos.
  7. Conclusion & Score — what done looks like, and the number that proves it is doing a good job.

Every agent has one of three guardrail styles: Autonomous (does the whole job itself), Approval required (prepares everything, acts only after your yes), or Hybrid escalation (runs the agreed process, escalates anything unusual to you).

Every agent is judged on two numbers: its goal metric (the target on its job card versus its current reading) and its accuracy (of the work you checked, how much was right). Accuracy bands steer the guardrails: below 70% means Approval required, 70 to 90% suggests Hybrid, 90%+ with a clean run makes it Autonomous-eligible. Tightening happens automatically; loosening is always your click. Each agent's panel also holds its full working prompt and a learning log that grows from your approval feedback.

How to use it

  1. Pick the tab that suits you. Customer Journey is the simplest place to start; the other tabs group your processes in different ways.
  2. Click any stage to open it, then add the workflows — the steps you follow for that part of the business.
  3. As you fill each stage in, the little bar shows how complete it is, so you can see what still needs writing down.
Tip: Start with the stage that causes the most headaches. Writing it down once saves you explaining it over and over.

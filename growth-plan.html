Joint tenancies and council tax

A house let by the room is an HMO for council tax and the owner pays (from 1 December 2023). Let the whole house to two tenants on one agreement of six months or more and they become liable. They claim Council Tax Reduction from the council (West Suffolk gives up to 100% on low income); any balance left is credited back to them so they are not out of pocket. Their Universal Credit housing costs do not change: each joint renter keeps their own 1-bed rate up to their share of the rent.

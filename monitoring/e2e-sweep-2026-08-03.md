last tuned: **`os-bplan` is gone and `kpi-library` is new**. That matches CLAUDE.md (Plan Builder
retired from the shell on 1 Aug 2026). The count is coincidentally still 27, so a count-only check
would have missed the swap. Enumerating live handled it correctly, and `kpi-library` was walked
and passed.

Also worth recording for the next tune: `javascript_tool` does **not** await a returned Promise,
so an `async` IIFE walking all tabs times out at 30s. The working pattern is fire-and-forget into
a global (`window.__sweep`) polled with plain synchronous reads. Timers are heavily throttled while
the Browser pane is hidden (~19s/tab); periodic screenshots wake the pane and speed the walk up.

### Two things this run learned about its own method

1. **A hidden badge still has text.** `#refreshingBadge` read "Refreshing… (showing data from
   12 min ago)" on two reads minutes apart while `#lastUpdated` advanced — which looks exactly
   like a stuck refresh. It was not. `setRefreshingIndicator()` (`js/dashboard.js:83-94`) hides
   the badge with `style.display='none'` and leaves the old `innerHTML` in place.
   `getComputedStyle().display` was `none` and `offsetParent` was `null`. Filed as
   `20260813-drift-monitor-119` so CHECK 4a asserts visibility before trusting badge text.

2. **The machine is not on UK time.** `Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone` reads
   `Europe/Paris`, UTC+2, so every locally-rendered timestamp in the app is an hour ahead of
   London (`#lastUpdated` 07:15 local = 06:15 London). `js/dashboard.js:1346` formats with
   `toLocaleTimeString('en-GB')` and no `timeZone`, so it follows the machine. Not filed as a
   defect — it is correct behaviour for a local render, and only 4 places in `js/` pin
   `Europe/London` explicitly. Recorded because it is the sort of thing that turns into a
   date-boundary bug later: 17 places in `js/` derive a date from `toISOString().slice(0,10)`
   (UTC) or local getters, and at UTC+2 those disagree with the London calendar day between
   midnight and 02:00 local. Worth a deliberate decision rather than a surprise.

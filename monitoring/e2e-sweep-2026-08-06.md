Two smaller timing observations worth folding into the skill later:
1. **`wealth` needs more than 2.6s to render.** At 2.6s it still read "Loading wealth data…" (246
   chars); at 12s it was fully populated (677,115 chars, 7/7 ✓). A short fixed wait would report a
   false FAIL on a healthy tab — same class of mistake as the old flat-20s auth wait.
2. **Health bars must be read after their deep checks settle.** `sitemap` read 3/7 mid-run and
   5/7 once complete; `income` and `costs` showed every check "Running…" on first read. Read the
   drawer twice, or wait for the re-run to finish, before quoting a ratio.
3. **Long `javascript_tool` evals time out when the browser pane is hidden.** Two multi-tab batches
   with cumulative waits over ~20s failed with "The Browser pane is currently hidden". A
   `computer{screenshot}` re-fronts the pane and restores normal eval behaviour. Keep per-eval waits
   short and batch small.

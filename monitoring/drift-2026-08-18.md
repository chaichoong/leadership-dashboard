opposite, and contradicts itself at `:548-551`. **Verified against live data**, which is what

and six Duckworth Building EICRs renewed **2026-03-08**. All are genuinely lapsed, and all
currently report as "no certificate on file". The red tile under-reads on a legal-obligation
surface. Filed `-189`.

**4 medium.** Block insurance and block GSC colour a row red but are counted on no tile
(`-190`, latent only — verified 0 live impact today: 1 Block property, and its 12 certificates
are 9 EICR / 2 EPC / 1 Emergency Lighting, no insurance and no GSC); an undated cert leaves the
row white while the SOP says white means everything is in date (`-191`); the agent-managed
"properties" count counts apartment rows (`-192`); the SOP's own header and changelog stopped
at v1.1 / 16 Apr 2026 while the registry says sopVer 1.13 (`-193`).

**2 low.** A rejected PAT is cleared from sessionStorage but left in the shared localStorage
